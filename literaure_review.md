# Literature Review
## Student Dropout Risk Prediction — 2026

### 1. Realinho et al. (2021) — Early Prediction of Student's Performance in Higher Education: A Case Study

This research investigates how machine-learning techniques can predict student academic performance and dropout risk in higher education. The study provides the foundation for the UCI Student Dropout and Academic Success dataset, which contains demographic, academic, and socioeconomic information. It demonstrates the potential of classification techniques for identifying students who may need academic support.

Source: https://doi.org/10.24432/C5MC89

### 2. Predicting Student Dropout and Academic Success (2022)

This research describes a dataset containing 4,424 student records and information about academic performance, enrollment, demographics, and socioeconomic conditions. It formulates student outcome prediction as a three-class classification problem: Dropout, Enrolled, and Graduate. The dataset provides a useful benchmark for comparing machine-learning algorithms.

Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2306-5729/7/11/146

### 3. Factors Influencing Academic Performance and Dropout Rates in Higher Education (2024)

This study reviews 95 research studies to identify factors associated with academic performance and university dropout. It identifies previous academic achievement, obtained credits, and student-related characteristics as important predictors. It also discusses the use of Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, and Neural Networks for educational prediction.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1080/03054985.2024.2316616

### 4. Artificial Intelligence-Driven Early Prediction of Student Dropout and Academic Outcomes in Higher Education (2026)

This research compares several machine-learning algorithms for predicting student academic outcomes. It reports that ensemble methods such as Random Forest and boosting algorithms can provide strong predictive performance. The study also highlights the importance of academic progress indicators, including approved curricular units and semester grades.

Source: https://publications.eai.eu/index.php/inis/article/view/11758

## Literature Review Summary

Previous studies demonstrate that machine learning can help identify students who may be at risk of dropping out. Academic progress, previous qualifications, enrollment information, and socioeconomic characteristics can provide useful predictive information. Based on these findings, this project will compare Logistic Regression and Random Forest to predict student academic outcomes.
